About KMH Engineering

About KMH Engineering

KMH Engineering is established to offer a wide range of best quality and technically competent structural engineering services. The KMH team, in close association with the client, seeks to provide the best quality services with time bound deliveries.

Specialized in "Fast Track" projects, we have a flexible approach to the particular requirements of each project and can tailor the services offered accordingly. With our design management experience gained over years, we provide a specialist, practical solutions to the discerning projects.

We have delivered some of the most enduring, high profile structures in various regions of the world such as Dubai, Abudhabi, Oman, Hongkong, Jordan & India. Several large and medium projects have been delivered in cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, and Bangalore in India.

KMH provides a comprehensive set of structural engineering services to support the design, construction, and improvement of building structures, working with the renowned architects, developers, owners, and builders to deliver structures that have been recognized as among the world’s best

As engineering leaders throughout each project phase, we guide the structural development process toward the most appropriate solutions

Why KMH Engineering ?

Projects are conceived with the idea of providing firmness, commodity and delight to it. Innovation, adaptability and expert critical execution have been the driving force of the company since its inception

An efficient design services consultancy for construction industry, where our client’s needs are prime focus for us. KMH is a place where we nurture talent to cater to progressive needs of our clients

Process of deliveries is crucially designed to increase productive thinking; we recruit people who have a passion to innovate. We train our team for ongoing technical expertise as well as to think beyond the limits.

We are highly client centric and are driven by passion towards our work; a young vibrant team is what we present to our clients to decipher their needs.
